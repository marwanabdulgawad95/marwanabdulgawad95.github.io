03 · About

How I work

“Nine years in, I have learned that project management is mostly about keeping promises: to clients, to teams, and to a budget.”

Every role I have held has been project management at its core. At the Red Sea Museum and the Intermix Residency, the projects were exhibitions and residencies. At ULTATEL, every SaaS client onboarding was a project with its own scope, timeline and go-live. At TECHNE, each incubator cycle and summit was a program to plan, staff and deliver. At INDEX.DEV and CallBlade, it was campaigns, pipelines and operations run against deadlines.

The method stays the same in every sector: define the scope early, build a realistic plan, manage vendors and stakeholders, catch risks before they become problems, and close out properly. The result is projects that land on time, on budget, and deliver what they were funded for.
